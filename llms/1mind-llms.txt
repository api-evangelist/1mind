# 1mind

> 1mind is an AI-Led Growth (AILG) platform that deploys multimodal AI Superhumans for enterprise revenue teams. Superhumans combine a face, a voice, and a GTM brain to qualify leads, deliver demos, handle objections, book meetings, and onboard customers — 24/7 across websites, in-product surfaces, video calls, and deal rooms. 1mind launched in November 2025 with $40M in total funding (Series A led by Battery Ventures). The company is the exclusive AI successor to Drift within the Clari + Salesloft Predictive Revenue System.

## Core Product

- [Home](https://www.1mind.com/): Overview of 1mind's Superhuman platform and flagship AI persona "Mindy," the company's primary demo and closer.
- [Customer Superhumans](https://www.1mind.com/customer-superhumans): Gallery of live Superhumans deployed by customers including Boston Dynamics, HubSpot, Seismic, New Relic, Phenom, Graylog, Pavilion, and others — each with distinct personas, goals, and CTA flows.
- [Announcements](https://www.1mind.com/announcements): Official launches and partnership news, including the $40M funding round, Clari + Salesloft exclusive AI successor designation, and the AI-Led Growth (AILG) platform launch.
- [Contact Us](https://www.1mind.com/contact-us): Primary inbound channel — visitors are encouraged to engage Mindy (the AI Superhuman) directly or submit a contact form for human follow-up.
